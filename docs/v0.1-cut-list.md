### Ordering

Select the same 4040 I-type slot 8 profile for all pieces. Request exact lengths rather than buying a long bar and cutting it yourself.

## Important mechanical note

The 400 mm internal board is intentionally aligned to the 400 mm playing surface. The final production CAD must add the exact bearing/rail mounting geometry after the selected linear rail family is frozen. Do not drill the extrusion based on this envelope model alone.

## Connector hardware

- 8 × 4040 L connector plates
- 8 × 4040 straight/connection plates
- 32 × M8 T-nuts, slot 8
- 32 × M8 socket-head screws
- 8 × adjustable feet or rubber feet

## Board support

Use a 400×400 mm rigid board panel. Recommended V0.1 construction is a 6–10 mm laminated plywood/MDF/composite panel mounted to the frame with removable fasteners. The final production version can replace this with a CNC-cut or printed surface.
