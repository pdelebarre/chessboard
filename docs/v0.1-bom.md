# Autonomous Chessboard V0.1 — BOM

## Design baseline

- 8×8 board
- 50 mm square pitch
- 400×400 mm playing surface
- 480×480 mm nominal 4040 outer frame
- Under-board CoreXY-style XY mechanism
- Passive magnetic chess pieces
- 64 Hall-effect sensing positions
- Raspberry Pi + ESP32 architecture

## Chess pieces

For V0.1, use commercially available inexpensive chess pieces and retrofit a standardized magnetic base, or print the pieces later. Each piece should have:

- 12 mm × 4 mm NdFeB disc magnet
- non-magnetic mechanical retention
- smooth low-friction underside
- consistent magnetic centre

Do not use magnets that are so strong that they cause pieces to interfere with neighbouring Hall sensors.
